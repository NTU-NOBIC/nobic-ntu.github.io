| 2-photon Random Access Mesoscope

Thorlabs 2-photom Random Access Mesoscope is designed  primarily for 2-photon in-vivo brain imaging in small animals with sub-cellular resolution. Tilting of the objective with respect to the vertical axis facilitates positioning of the imaged animal.

Available techniques:

  • fast 2-photon excited florescence imaging (using a resonant scanner)

Objectives:

Custom mesoscopic water immersion objective

  • Field of view diameter: 5 mm
  • Excitation NA: 0.6
  • Emission NA: 1.0

Fluorescence excitation sources:

Spectra Physics InSight X3 DeepSee tuneable fs-laser (usable range: 900 nm - 1070 nm)

Detectors and cameras:

  • 2x GaAsP photomultiplier tube

Emission filters:

  • GFP
  • RFP

Software:

  • ScanImage

Other features:

  • Resonant scanner for fast imaging
  • Objective rotation +/- 20° from the vertical axes
  • Remote focusing for fast fast focus changes
  • Motorised stage
  • Motorised focus

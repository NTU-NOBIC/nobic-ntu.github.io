| Custom Microscope for Laser-Induced DNA damage



A custom-built microscope equipped with a 405 nm laser scanning system enabling targeted sample illumination. The primary application is induction of DNA damage within selected regions of the nucleus; however, the microscope could be used for other types of sample stimulation too. The system is based on an inverted microscope with a CMOS camera for widefield imaging. A 405 nm laser beam is scanned to position a sub-micron focus anywhere within a part of the objective’s field of view.

Available techniques:

  • Brightfield and widefield epifluorescence imaging
  • Controlled laser irradiation for inducing DNA damage or other sample stimulation

Objectives:

  • LUCPLFLN 20x/0.45 dry, FWD 6.6-7.8 mm, CG 0-2.0 mm
    [FWD = free working distance, CG = cover glass]

Other Olympus objectives can be installed as required by the applications.

DNA damage laser line:

  • 405 nm (laser power at the sample)

Light sources:

  • halogen lamp (transmitted light)
  • metal-halide lamp (epifluorescence

Filter sets:

  • Laser illumination and reflected brightfield (---; 50/50 beamsplitter; 420LP)
  • FITC epifluorescence (480/40; 510; 535/50)
  • Cy3 epifluorescence (545/25; 565; 605/70)

Detectors and cameras:

  • Basler daA3840-45um monochrome CMOS (3860x2178 pixels, 2 µm/pixel)

Software:

  • Custom software to control the camera and define lines and speed of laser scanning
